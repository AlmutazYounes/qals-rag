1. Chunk Size Dilemma

Small chunks match with high precision but lose surrounding context. TopoRAG indexes micro propositions and expands upward to meso parents.

2. Hubness and Anisotropy

Dense vectors cluster in narrow cones, creating artificial "hub" vectors that dominate search results. TopoRAG calibrates Riemannian local density.

3. Fixed-k Retrieval Trap

Hardcoded top-k injects noise into simple queries and starves complex ones. TopoRAG uses dynamic score-knee and entropy cutoff.
